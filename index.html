IATS Student Data Privacy Policy

Institute of Advanced Technology & Studies • Version 2.0

1. Purpose & Scope

IATS CONNECT is dedicated strictly to scholarly collaboration, study partner connections, and institutional announcements. We never sell, monetize, or disclose student records to third parties or advertising brokers.

2. Collected Academic Data

Data stored comprises student names, verified personal contact emails, registered course syllabus codes, faculty affiliations, and academic study group postings in the secure Cloud Firestore cluster.

3. Direct Message Confidentiality

Scholar-to-scholar direct communications are private between connected study partners. Conversations are preserved exclusively to ensure continuous academic collaboration across campus devices.

4. Student Data Rights & Deletion

Any registered student may update their coursework, change study spot preferences, or request permanent deletion of their account and associated records by contacting the IATS System Administrator.

Academic Terms of Service & Conduct

Institute of Advanced Technology & Studies Academic Code

1. Purely Academic Mandate & Zero-Tolerance Policy

IATS CONNECT is established exclusively for higher learning, academic revision, research cooperation, and peer study partner discovery. Any use of this platform for non-academic solicitation is strictly prohibited.

2. Strict Prohibition of Adult Content & Pornography

There is an absolute, zero-tolerance prohibition against any pornographic, sexually explicit, lewd, or suggestive text, links, or media. Real-time automated content filters immediately intercept and reject non-compliant submissions.

3. Academic Integrity & Anti-Cheating

Collaborative discussion of lecture topics is encouraged; however, posting unauthorized exam keys or engaging in exam impersonation violates the IATS Academic Disciplinary Statute.

4. Respectful Peer Engagement

Scholars must maintain professional, respectful decorum across all course discussion threads and direct messaging channels. Bullying, hate speech, or harassment will result in immediate suspension.
